# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
- **Primary**: College students (在校学生) actively using campus life
- **Context**: Browsing and trading idle items between classes, selling items before graduation, finding affordable textbooks and electronics
- **Jobs**: Sell unwanted items quickly, find good deals on secondhand goods, trust seller identities, discover interesting items

### Brand Personality
- **Voice**: 活力环保型 (Active & Eco-friendly)
- **3-word personality**: 活力 (Active), 环保 (Eco-conscious), 值得信赖 (Trustworthy)
- **Emotional goals**: Sense of community participation, environmental consciousness, excitement of finding good deals, trust in campus community

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone**: Bold and energetic with fresh blue-green palette
- **Theme**: Light mode with vibrant accents (NOT dark mode - this is for daytime campus browsing)
- **References**: 咸鱼/转转 (Xianyu/Zhuanzhuan) - established second-hand marketplace aesthetics
- **Anti-references**: Generic AI-generated UIs, overly minimal sterile designs, dated enterprise software look

### Design Principles
1. **Trust through transparency** - Clear pricing, verified badges, seller ratings visible at a glance
2. **Eco-conscious messaging** - Environmental impact stats (items saved from landfill), green color accents
3. **Youthful energy** - Bold colors, dynamic micro-interactions, playful but not childish
4. **Campus community** - School-specific identity, student verification badges, peer trust signals
5. **Efficient browsing** - Card-based layouts optimized for rapid scanning, lazy-loaded images
